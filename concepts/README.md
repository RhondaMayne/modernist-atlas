# Concepts

The Concepts section of the Modernist Atlas contains the key theoretical ideas, critical terms, and recurring themes used to study literary modernism. Rather than summarising individual articles or books, these pages synthesise ideas from multiple readings to provide a broader understanding of each concept.

Each concept note includes:

- **Definition** – a concise explanation of the concept.
- **Why it Matters** – its significance within modernist studies.
- **Key Questions** – questions that guide further reading and analysis.
- **Key Thinkers** – major scholars associated with the concept.
- **Primary Texts** – literary works in which the concept is particularly significant.
- **Related Concepts** – connections to other ideas within the Atlas.
- **Atlas Notes** – links to article notes and other entries that develop the concept.
