# Notes

The Notes section contains the research notes that underpin the Modernist Atlas. It brings together critical reading, analysis, and synthesis, documenting both modernist scholarship and the ideas that emerge through sustained engagement with it.

The section is organised into two complementary collections:

## Articles

Article notes record individual scholarly sources, including journal articles, book chapters, and critical essays. Each note captures the author's central argument, key concepts, strongest evidence, critical evaluation, and connections to wider debates within modernist studies. Together, these notes provide a structured and searchable record of modernist criticism.

## Permanent

Permanent notes capture ideas that extend beyond a single source. They synthesise concepts, arguments, observations, and recurring themes that emerge across multiple readings. These notes enable the Atlas to develop into an interconnected body of knowledge rather than a collection of isolated summaries.

Together, these sections preserve both the critical conversation surrounding literary modernism and the conceptual knowledge that emerges from it.
